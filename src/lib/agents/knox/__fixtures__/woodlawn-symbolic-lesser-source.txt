Behind Greater Woodlawn Federal Credit Union's Fees

An important benefit of Greater Woodlawn Federal Credit Union's membership is that most GWFCU fees and charges are lower than comparable ones at other financial institutions. They're designed to recover only the approximate overall cost to the membership for the services provided.
