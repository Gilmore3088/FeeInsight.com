OVERDRAFT FEE
Overdraft fees are not charged on this account. In most cases, we will decline or return transactions that will overdraw your account. However, there
may still be times when your account could have a negative balance. This could happen in the following ways:
● When your debit card is authorized for one amount, the final charge is more than the amount of money in your account (for example, adding a tip
at a restaurant or pumping gas).
● A scheduled payment is deducted when you have insufficient funds in your account.
● When you get charged a service charge, such as a maintenance service charge and the current balance is less than the maintenance service charge.
In these situations, the transactions will process and result in a negative balance on your account, but we will not charge you an overdraft fee.
CHECKS
This is a checkless account. Do not order or write checks for this account. Checks attempting to process through this account will be returned
unpaid. Electronic checks, or checks processed through Bill Pay will be paid.
