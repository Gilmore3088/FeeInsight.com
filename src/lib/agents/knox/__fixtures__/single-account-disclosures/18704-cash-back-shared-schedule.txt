Fees for Overdrawing Account:
Fees may be imposed on each check, draft, item, ATM card withdrawal, debit card point of purchase, preauthorized automatic debit,

account balance. The entire balance in your account may not be available for withdrawal, transfer or paying a check, draft or item.
