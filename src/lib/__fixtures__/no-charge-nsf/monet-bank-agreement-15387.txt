(iii) Returned Items.
If an item drafted by you (such as a check) or a transaction you set up (such as a
preauthorized transfer) is presented for payment in an amount that is more than the
amount of money in your Account, and we decide not to pay the item or transaction, we
will return the item unpaid. We do not currently charge a Nonsufficient Funds (NSF) Fee
for returning an item unpaid. If we change this practice in the future, we will provide you
with advance notice as required by applicable law.
