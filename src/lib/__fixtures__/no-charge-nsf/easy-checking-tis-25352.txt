Easy Checking incurs a $10.00 service charge per statement cycle. We will make a monthly account statement available for your checking
account during each statement cycle. The statement cycle may or may not be a calendar month, but typically it will not be more than 32 days or
less than 28 days. The specific dates covered by your account statement will be disclosed on your statement.
This account incurs a $5.00 paper statement fee per statement cycle, if you choose to receive paper statements. To avoid this fee, you can sign
up for electronic statements (E-Statements)
OVERDRAFT AND NONSUFFICIENT FUNDS (NSF) FEES: In your Easy Checking account, we do not charge an NSF fee or Overdraft
fee for ATM, Debit Card, Checks or ACH transactions because we typically do not authorize or approve these types of transactions if sufficient
funds are not available to pay these items.
