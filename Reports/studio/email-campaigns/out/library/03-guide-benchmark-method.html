How to benchmark a fee schedule

The method behind our reports, in four steps you can run yourself.

The method

1.Pick a real peer group. Same charter type and the institutions your customers actually compare you with.
2.Compare like with like. Same fee, same channel, same conditions.
3.Place yourself by percentile. Below the 25th percentile is low; above the 75th is high.
4.Flag outliers with one rule. More than 2× the peer median, or more than 1.5× the interquartile range above the 75th percentile. Outliers get a written reason or a review.

Common mistakes

□Benchmarking against national banks when customers compare you with the credit union down the street.
□Comparing your branch price with a competitor's online price.
□Using an old schedule instead of the one published today.
□Recording "waived with direct deposit" as $0. Record the condition.
