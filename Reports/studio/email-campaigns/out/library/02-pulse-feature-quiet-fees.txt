The fees nobody benchmarks (customers notice them anyway)
=========================================================

Everyone benchmarks overdraft. Few institutions benchmark the fees a customer pays once a year, and those come up in a branch argument or a one-star review.

1. Debit card replacement. Check whether a standard reissue is priced like a rush order. See the market (https://feeinsight.com/fees/card_replacement?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=library&utm_content=pulse-feature-quiet-fees).
2. Deposited item returned. The customer who deposited a bad check didn't write it. See the market (https://feeinsight.com/fees/deposited_item_return?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=library&utm_content=pulse-feature-quiet-fees).
3. Stop payment. A lower online price is an easy way to stand out. See the market (https://feeinsight.com/fees/stop_payment?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=library&utm_content=pulse-feature-quiet-fees).
4. Cashier's check. Often priced once years ago and never revisited. See the market (https://feeinsight.com/fees/cashiers_check?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=library&utm_content=pulse-feature-quiet-fees).
