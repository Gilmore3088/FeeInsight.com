The fees nobody benchmarks (customers notice them anyway)

Everyone benchmarks overdraft. Few institutions benchmark the fees a customer pays once a year, and those come up in a branch argument or a one-star review.

1.Debit card replacement. Check whether a standard reissue is priced like a rush order. See the market.
2.Deposited item returned. The customer who deposited a bad check didn't write it. See the market.
3.Stop payment. A lower online price is an easy way to stand out. See the market.
4.Cashier's check. Often priced once years ago and never revisited. See the market.
