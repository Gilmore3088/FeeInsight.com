The number that matters

14% of institutions in the index publish a $0 monthly maintenance fee on at least one checking account, and 52% charge $5 or less. If your entry-level checking account still carries a fee, the "free checking" competitor is closer than you think.

One idea to use this month

List your checking accounts by monthly fee and by how to waive it. If the waiver needs more than one condition, a customer will read it as "not free". Try rewriting it as one sentence.
