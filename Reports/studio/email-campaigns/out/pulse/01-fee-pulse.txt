FEE PULSE · OCTOBER 6, 2026

This month in the Bank Fee Index
================================

* $30 — median overdraft (1,324 institutions)
* $30 — median NSF (1,630 institutions)
* $5.50 — median monthly maintenance (530 institutions)


THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS

The typical NSF fee ($30) is the same as the typical overdraft fee ($30), even though NSF is the fee regulators have pushed hardest on re-presented items. If your NSF fee sits above $31.50, you're in the most expensive quarter of the market on the fee examiners ask about first.


[ ONE IDEA TO USE THIS MONTH ]
Check whether your schedule charges NSF on re-presented items, and how many times. If the answer takes more than one sentence, so will the conversation with your examiner.
