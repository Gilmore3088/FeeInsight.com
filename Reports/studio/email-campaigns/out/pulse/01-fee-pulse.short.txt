Returned-deposit fees: the middle half spans $16
================================================

When a customer deposits a check that bounces, the middle half of the banks and credit unions in the index list a fee between $9 and $25. That spans $16, compared with $8 for overdraft ($25 to $33). The figures come from 1,821 institutions that publish a returned-deposit fee.

Overdraft (paid item): $25 to $33, median $30

NSF (returned item): $25 to $32, median $30

Stop payment: $22 to $30, median $27.50

Deposited item returned: $9 to $25, median $15

Middle half (25th to 75th percentile) with the median marked. Bank Fee Index, national, October 17, 2026. One value per institution, from its own published fee schedule. Institutions behind each fee: Overdraft (paid item) 2,500, NSF (returned item) 2,668, Stop payment 3,193, Deposited item returned 1,821.
