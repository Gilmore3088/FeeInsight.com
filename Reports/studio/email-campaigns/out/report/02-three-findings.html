Competitive Fee Position · 2 of 3

Three findings that show up in almost every report

After building these reports across banks and credit unions of every size, the same three issues come up again and again. Check yours against them today.

1. One fee is far out of line with the rest

Most institutions price close to the market on most fees, then have one or two at 2× the peer median or more. Card replacement is the usual suspect (national spread $7.63 to $43.75), followed by returned deposits and stop payments.

2. NSF is still on the schedule while peers dropped it

A quarter of banks in the index charge $7.11 or less for NSF. If yours is $30 or more, you're in the expensive quarter on the fee examiners and journalists ask about first.

3. The schedule no longer matches the website

The PDF says one thing and the product page says another, or the online price isn't listed at all. It's the cheapest finding to fix and the most embarrassing to have a customer find.

10-minute self-check

□Any fee above 2× the peer median? (Use the medians in the sample report.)
□NSF above $30? On re-presented items too?
□Do the fee schedule PDF and the website agree, line by line?
