======================================================

Open the sample report: https://feeinsight.com/reports/sample-competitive-fee-position?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=report&utm_content=read-your-sample
No login. Every figure links to the source schedule.

The report is built to be read by a busy executive. If you only have five minutes, read these in order:

1. Executive summary. Three findings, each with a dollar figure and a peer count.
2. Position chart. Every headline fee placed between the peer low 25% and high 25%. Dots outside the band are the story.
3. Outlier flags. Fees more than 2× the peer median, or statistically high. Each gets a suggested next step.
4. Named competitors. The same fees at the institutions customers actually compare you to.
5. Sources. Every number links to the published document, with its date, so nobody in the room has to take it on faith.


[ WHAT "PEER COHORT" MEANS HERE ]
Same charter type and the same asset-size tier, drawn from the 1,232 institutions in the index. That's why the medians in a report differ from the national numbers. The national NSF median is $25, but bank and credit union medians are $18 and $25.

In three days: the three findings that show up in almost every report we build.
