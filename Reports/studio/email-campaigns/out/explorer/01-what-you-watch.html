You're set up. Here's what you'll hear about

When a fee schedule you follow changes, we tell you what changed, by how much, and where that puts it against peers. No noise in between.

How to read an institution profile

□The fee table: each fee next to the peer median. Arrows mark above or below.
□Source links: the published document and the date we read it.
□Peer group: institutions of the same charter type and similar size.
□Coverage note: which fees the schedule didn't publish. A missing fee isn't a $0 fee.

While you wait for the first alert, a good place to start is your state: state fee benchmarks and the National Fee Index.
