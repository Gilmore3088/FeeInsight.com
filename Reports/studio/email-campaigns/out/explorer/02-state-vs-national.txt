Hold your state up against the national line
============================================

State pages on Fee Insight show local medians. To know if they're high, you need the national line to compare against. Here it is for the four fees people search most:

Fee | low 25% | median | high 25% | banks | CUs | n
Overdraft (paid item) | $9 | $29 | $34 | $30 | $25 | 88
NSF (returned item) | $10 | $25 | $30 | $18 | $25 | 173
Monthly maintenance | $2 | $5 | $15 | $8 | $5 | 133
Out-of-network ATM | $1 | $2 | $3 | $2 | $1.50 | 69
Bank Fee Index, October 3, 2026. One headline amount per institution (lowest published amount for that fee). "Low 25%" and "High 25%" are the 25th and 75th percentiles; n = institutions with that fee published. Banks and CUs columns are medians.

In smaller states a median can rest on a handful of institutions. Check the n on the state page. Under 10, treat the number as a signal, not a verdict.
