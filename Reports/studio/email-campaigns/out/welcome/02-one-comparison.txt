One comparison to make before your next pricing meeting
=======================================================

Pick one fee your customers notice, such as overdraft or NSF, and place it against your own state rather than the country.


[ MAKE THE COMPARISON ]
1. Open your state's page (https://feeinsight.com/research?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=one-comparison) and find the fee.
2. Note the median and the middle 50%, the range between the 25th and 75th percentiles.
3. Check how many institutions stand behind the number. If only a few, check their schedules directly.
4. Write your own price next to it: below, inside or above the middle 50%.

This gives your committee a starting point. Hamilton then names the institutions that sit next to you, fee by fee.
