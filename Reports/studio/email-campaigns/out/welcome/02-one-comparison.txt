One comparison to make before your next pricing meeting
=======================================================

Pick one fee your customers notice, such as overdraft or NSF, and place it against your own state rather than the country.


[ MAKE THE COMPARISON ]
1. Open your state's page and find the fee. Find your state (https://feeinsight.com/research?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=one-comparison).
2. Note the median and the middle half (the 25th to 75th percentile: the middle 50% of institution benchmark values).
3. Check how many institutions stand behind the number. A small count is a hint, not a verdict.
4. Write your own price next to it: below, inside or above the middle half.

This gives your committee a starting point. The state view doesn't show which institutions sit next to you; Hamilton Pro does.
