Where to find the fee data for your market
==========================================

Thanks for subscribing. The fee benchmarks come from institutions' published schedules, and the views below need no login. Start with the one closest to the institutions you compete with.

- Your state: each headline fee in your state next to the national median, banks and credit unions side by side. Find your state (https://feeinsight.com/research).

- Your Fed district: the same view across a district. Open the district reports (https://feeinsight.com/reports).

- One institution: its fee table, the schedule it came from, and the date we read it. Search institutions (https://feeinsight.com/institutions).
