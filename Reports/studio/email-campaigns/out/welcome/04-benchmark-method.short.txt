Benchmark any fee schedule in 30 minutes
[ THE METHOD ]
1. Pick a real peer group. Same charter type (bank or credit union) and a similar asset size. Comparing a $300M credit union with a national bank tells you nothing.
2. Use the headline amount. For each fee, take the lowest published price on the schedule. That's what a customer can get, and what a competitor will quote.
3. Place yourself by percentile. Sort the peers' prices. Where do you land? Below the 25th percentile is low, above the 75th is high.
4. Flag outliers with one rule. A fee is an outlier if it's more than 2× the peer median, or more than 1.5× the interquartile range above the 75th percentile. Outliers get a written reason or a review.
