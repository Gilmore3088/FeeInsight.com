Peer group, percentile, outlier rule. The steps behind our reports, free.

Benchmark any fee schedule in 30 minutes
[ THE METHOD ]
1. Pick a real peer group. Same charter type (bank or credit union) and the institutions your customers actually compare you to. A $300M credit union and a national bank tell you nothing about each other.
2. Compare like with like. Same fee, same channel, same conditions. A branch price against an online price isn't a comparison.
3. Place yourself by percentile. Sort the peers' prices. Below the 25th percentile is low; above the 75th is high.
4. Flag outliers with one rule. A fee is an outlier if it's more than 2× the peer median, or more than 1.5× the interquartile range above the 75th percentile. Outliers get a written reason or a review.
