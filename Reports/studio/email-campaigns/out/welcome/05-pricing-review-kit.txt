Run a fee review your committee will actually finish

Last email in the series. Here's a one-page agenda you can paste into your next ALCO, pricing or product committee meeting. It turns the last four emails into decisions.


[ FEE REVIEW AGENDA (60 MINUTES) ]
1. Position (15 min): our headline fees against the market's 25th percentile, median and 75th percentile. List every fee above the 75th.
2. Revenue (10 min): service charges on deposit accounts from our Call Report (Schedule RI) or NCUA 5300 fee income. Which 3 fees drive most of it?
3. Risk (10 min): re-presentment NSF, authorize-positive/settle-negative, overdraft caps, Reg E opt-in records.
4. Customer (10 min): complaints and reviews that mention a fee by name in the last 12 months.
5. Decide (15 min): for each high-quarter fee, keep (with a written reason), cut, restructure (cap, de minimis, online price), or study further.

Two outcomes make a review worth it: a fee you can defend in one sentence, and a list of fees you'll stop defending.
