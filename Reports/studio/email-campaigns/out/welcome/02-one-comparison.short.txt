One comparison to make before your next pricing meeting
=======================================================

Pick one fee your customers notice, such as overdraft or NSF, and place it against your own state rather than the country.

[ MAKE THE COMPARISON ]
1. Open your state's page and find the fee. Find your state (https://feeinsight.com/research).
2. Note the median and the middle 50% (the 25th to 75th percentile: the middle 50% of institutions list a fee in this range).
3. Check how many institutions stand behind the number. If only a few do, treat it as a lead to check against their schedules.
4. Write your own price next to it: below, inside or above the middle 50%.

This gives your committee a starting point. Hamilton takes the next step: the named institutions that sit next to you, fee by fee.
