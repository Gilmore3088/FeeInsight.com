Where to find the fee data for your market
==========================================

Thanks for subscribing. The fee benchmarks come from institutions' published schedules, and the views below need no login. Start with the one closest to the institutions you compete with.

- Your state: each headline fee in your state next to the national median, banks and credit unions side by side. Find your state (https://feeinsight.com/research?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- Your Fed district: the same view across a district. Open the district reports (https://feeinsight.com/reports?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- One institution: its fee table, the schedule it came from, and the date we read it. Search institutions (https://feeinsight.com/institutions?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- One fee across the country: the national median and middle 50%, with banks and credit unions compared. Browse fees (https://feeinsight.com/fees?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).

What's free: every fee's own page, with its national median and middle 50%, and the national index table for the headline fees, including overdraft, NSF and out-of-network ATM. The rest of the index table and breakdowns by asset size come with a Hamilton Pro plan.

Explore the free fee benchmarks: https://feeinsight.com/research?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data
