Where to find the fee data for your market
==========================================

Thanks for subscribing. Every figure on the site comes from a bank's or credit union's own published fee schedule, and every view below is free with no login. Start with the one closest to the institutions you compete with.

- Your state: each headline fee in your state next to the national median, banks and credit unions side by side. Find your state (https://feeinsight.com/research?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- Your Fed district: the same view across a district. Open the district reports (https://feeinsight.com/reports?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- One institution: its fee table, the schedule it came from, and the date we read it. Search institutions (https://feeinsight.com/institutions?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
- One fee across the country: the median and middle half for any fee. Browse fees (https://feeinsight.com/fees?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome&utm_content=find-the-data).
