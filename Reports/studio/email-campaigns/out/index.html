17 emails across 6 automations. Each file: .html for MailerLite custom HTML, .txt for the plain-text version.

Trigger: Joins group 'Fee Insight · Report requests' after confirming (sources: report_national, report_district, capture_report_sample, capture_homepage). Not 'report' (paid quote requests). Skips anyone in Pro customers.

Explorers: what you follow, then the monthly snapshot

Trigger: Joins group 'Fee Insight · Watchers' (sources: capture_state, capture_institution). One email; the monthly state or national snapshot follows. Skips anyone in Report requests or Pro customers.
