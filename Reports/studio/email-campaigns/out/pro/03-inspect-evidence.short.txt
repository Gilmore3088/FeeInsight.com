Check the source behind a figure
================================

Before a number goes in front of your committee, you should be able to show where it came from. Each fee on an institution's public profile links to the schedule page it was read from, and the profile shows the date we last read that institution.

[ TRY IT ONCE ]
1. Open Hamilton and pick a fee where you and your peers differ.
2. Find the competitor whose fee is furthest from yours, open its public profile on feeinsight.com, and follow the fee schedule link.
3. If the schedule disagrees with what we show, reply with the institution and fee. We re-check it.
