CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE— 1999–2000 REGULAR SESSION

Assembly Bill
No. 1606

Introduced  by  Assembly Member Margett

February 26, 1999

An act to add Section 3351 to the Financial Code, relating to banks.


LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGEST


AB 1606, as amended, Margett. Banks: processing of checks and deposits.
Existing law establishes the Department of Financial Institutions, which regulates banks and trust companies, savings associations, credit unions, and industrial loan companies.
Existing law sets forth various regulations and requirements with respect to the operation of banks in this state.
This bill would require banks organized under the laws of this state to clear checks and credit deposits in the order that they are received. The bill would limit the fee or charge for a returned check or deposit item to $15. The bill would prohibit a bank from honoring or otherwise paying funds relative to any check that exceeds the amount available for withdrawal from an account because the account contains a hold on funds.

Bill Text

The people of the State of California do enact as follows:


SECTION 1.

 Section 3351 is added to the Financial Code, to read:

3351.
 (a) No bank organized under the laws of this state shall, on a daily or other periodic basis, process and clear checks drawn upon an account prior to crediting that account with any particular deposit made prior to the bank’s receipt of any particular check drawn upon that account. Both checks drawn against an account and deposits to that account shall be cleared in the order received.
(b) The maximum fee or charge that a bank may levy against a depositor for a returned or dishonored check or a returned deposit item shall not exceed the sum of fifteen dollars ($15).
(c) The amount of any fee or charge for dishonored or returned checks or deposit items that is established by a bank pursuant to the limitations of subdivision (b), for accounts that receive deposits drawn on the United States government or accounts that are so-called basic or no frills accounts, shall not exceed the amount of the same fee or charge established for all other accounts of the bank.

(d)A bank shall not honor or otherwise pay out funds with respect to any check during any period in which the amount of the check exceeds the unrestricted amount available for withdrawal from an account because the account, in whole or in part, is subject to a hold on funds. Any check held by the bank in accordance with the preceding sentence shall be processed for payment whenever the hold on funds is lifted or otherwise terminated.
