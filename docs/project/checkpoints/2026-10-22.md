### The method that ran
- Each change: a builder PR, then an independent review of the exact head, then fixes in the
  same PR, then a re-review, then merge with a receipt naming the head SHA and both verdicts.
- Admit-only: no Knox change holds, rejects or re-files a fee that publishes today, so no Darwin
  re-check or dry-run count applied. #2543, #2545, #2549 and #2550 rename some reads at the same
  category and price.
- Read diffs against base: #2547 to #2550 each diffed their reads over every Texas, seven-state
  and shadow-lab text. #2543 relied on the reviewer's probe of 2,213 answer-key reads. #2545 and
  #2546 had no full read diff.
- Every PR from #2545 to #2550 had at least one blocking review round, all fixed before merge.
  In five of them the blocker was a path for a wrong fee to trace or publish:
  - #2545: the shared check traced any category named "Safe Deposit Box <size>".
  - #2547: a cash-advance limit line and received ACH files read as fees.
  - #2548: two blocking rounds (a column heading carried past a section heading, then past a
    self-named fee); a third path (a headless third pair borrowing the right heading) was found
    in a non-blocking round and fixed before merge.
  - #2549: stacked wires under an international heading published as domestic, first in Knox,
    then in the shared check.
  - #2550: glued-sentence cuts dropped qualifiers (rush, international, tier, non-member,
    business, foreign) and published wrong-meaning fees. Fixed with a positive rule: every
    dropped word must be filler or the kept name's own word, at the cost of three gains.
  - #2546's blocker was different: the finding claimed a self-check on the stored text that
    actually ran on the split text. The probe reads it found did not publish (Darwin dropped
    them), and the fix moved the self-check onto the stored text.
- What it taught: a new read path is wrong until a reviewer has tried to break it with
  live-style near misses, and every such probe text becomes a failing-first test at read level
  and through `runCase`. A claimed safeguard must be checked in the code, not the write-up. The
  details are in the findings: `2026-10-22-box-sizes-named-by-size-alone.md`,
  `-tier-column-rows-read-first-cell-only.md`, `-held-unclassified-at-the-right-price.md`,
  `-column-heading-names-second-fee.md`, `-stacked-cell-sibling-variants.md` and
  `-live-audit-replay.md`.
