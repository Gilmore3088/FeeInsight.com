# Research journeys must restore their own financial fixture baseline

**Cause:** the browser suite seeds Hamilton's common fixtures once in global setup. The command-center journey then deletes the home institution's and peers' single financial records and inserts its own quarterly series. A later research journey inherited that series even though its literal peer criteria assume the common home's $9 billion assets. Serial execution prevents simultaneous writes; it does not restore another journey's baseline.

**Evidence:** the combined diagnostic reached research after the command-center tests and displayed selection bounds of $5,103,624,500 and $20,414,498,000 instead of the baseline's half-to-double range. `command-center-journey.pw.ts` explicitly replaces those financial records. `fixtures.ts` defines the home as 9,000,000 USD thousands, and `formatAssetBound` displays the exact baseline bounds as `$4.5B` and `$18B`; full-dollar output was a consequence of the different input, not a new expected answer.

**Fix:** research calls the existing loopback-only `seedHamiltonFixtures` in its own `beforeAll`, closing its database connection afterward. That restores the complete common fixture baseline before its serial tests. The literal asset limits, fee values, peer membership, lineage and account-boundary assertions remain in place. No production data or product behavior changes.

**Validation:** the research-only diagnostic reused the existing production build, with verification disabled, and passed all ten tests in 27 seconds. It covered peer selection and refinement, zero/stale/missing/rate fees, save and reopen, PDF download, cross-account and unsubscribed refusal, provider-off refusal and zero recorded paid calls. The earlier failed artifacts and logs were preserved. This isolated diagnostic does not establish acceptance for the changed candidate; the complete combined journey run remains a final verification requirement.

**Related database isolation:** the vault-retention suite owns temporary source and fee tables on one connection, but its optional lineage discovery also found public tables through the default search path. On the used pipeline fixture database, 135 `public.pipeline_feedback` rows cited document IDs in the temporary suite's 1–21 range. The unchanged suite failed all three cases even when rerun alone: the expected one eligible object and thirteen objects after removing the test's lineage both became zero. Restricting only that test session to `pg_temp` made all three cases pass on the same used database. The thirteen lineage cases, off-mode no-change assertion and explicit fake-S3 deletion assertion remain intact. Atlas's production retention policy, live switch and lineage discovery are unchanged.

**Guard:** `tests/e2e/hamilton/research-journey.pw.ts` restores the baseline before its real browser assertions. The suite remains one worker with serial research tests. `src/lib/agents/atlas/vault-retention.db.test.ts` confines its temporary fixture session's lookup path and retains every original behavioral assertion. Tracked with Foundation #1048 in PR #2995.
