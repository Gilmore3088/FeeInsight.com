## 1. What it felt like

**Consumer, anonymous.** The guide is the best page on the site. Landing cold on `/guides/overdraft-fees` I understood within a second what it was about, and the number I came for — the $32.56 median — is inside the "Check your own bank" box at 409px on desktop and 487px on mobile, well above the fold, with a big "Find your institution" button next to it. The guide reads as a sequence (six anchored sections, a table of contents, a threshold table at the end that tells me whether I am paying more than 75% of the country). Then the journey broke. On `/institutions?fee=overdraft` I typed "Rainier", the dropdown showed the bank, and on desktop I could not click it: the suggestion list paints underneath the "Browse by geography" section, so my click hit the map heading (Playwright: `<section class="fi-reveal …"> intercepts pointer events`; on mobile the tap is intercepted the same way). Keyboard arrow/Enter did nothing either. When I forced my way through, I landed on `/institution/3` with the `fee=overdraft` context gone, the overdraft row 2,480px (desktop) / 5,985px (mobile) below the top, un-highlighted, and the page opening with "Verified fees · tier_4 · EVIDENCE SNAPSHOT · SOURCE Found · EXTRACTION Rows detected" — a data-ops dashboard, not an answer to "does my bank charge more than $32.56?". (The one saving grace: a small "Overdraft fee: $34.96 — 7% above the national median" chip does exist further down, on every institution page.) From the institution page there is no way back to the guide and nothing about alerts; the only related link is the global "Guides" nav item. The "Create a free account" CTA took me to a form titled "Get access to bank fee benchmarking data" asking for *Work email*, *Institution / Company*, *Type* and *Your role* — nothing about the overdraft alert I clicked for; `intent`/`category` are in the URL only and are not echoed as hidden fields or copy. Submitting gives "Registration failed. Please try again." (POST 200, Stripe absent — environment limit, but the message would be the same for any real failure). `/guides` splits consumer vs "For Bankers & Consultants" clearly, and the gated pro guide is honest: a lock, the guide's own summary, "View Plans", and an explicit line that consumer guides stay free.

**Professional.** From `/` I found "Pricing" in one click on desktop — but only because the landing page has *no global header*; the Pricing link is a body button at y=478, and on mobile it sits at y=875, below the 812px fold, so it's scroll-then-click. `/for-institutions` promises "Four tools. One subscription." and shows hard numbers: **49 FEE CATEGORIES**, "12+ institutions". `/subscribe` shows two clean plan cards ($499.99/mo per seat, $5,000/yr per seat, "Save $1,000") and claims "every fee category we benchmark, 8,000+ institutions" — no category count on the pricing page itself, but 49 is claimed on `/`, `/for-institutions` and `/login`, while the gates on `/fees/overdraft` and the pro guide say "60,000+ fee observations from 8,000+ institutions" and the home stats say 384 verified observations from 12 institutions. Four different scale stories on one visit. `/fees/overdraft` signed out is generous — median/percentiles/range, the distribution chart, the district table — and the gate is a single well-labelled lock card ("Detailed breakdown by charter, tier, and state") with "View Plans". `/pro` signed out redirects to `/login?from=%2Fpro`, a username/password form (it says *Username*, the register form collected an *email*).

**Registered consumer (signed in).** The "Your institutions / What you pay for overdraft (od)" panel appears ~475ms after navigation start on both viewports, above the fold, with Cascadia $33.60 (+$1.04) and Rainier $34.96 (+$2.40) and deep links that actually scroll to the highlighted row (`/institution/3?fee=overdraft#fee-overdraft` → row lands at 96px). It is inserted above the hero box, so the page shifts (CLS 0.083 desktop). The nav corner shows a black "C" avatar + "Account"; Sign in / Get Pro Access are gone. `/account` is confirmed a professional dashboard: "FREE ACCOUNT", an "Unlock the full platform… View Plans" card (the $499.99 figure is one click further, on `/subscribe`), an "Organization profile — Complete profile" block, and quick actions for Peer Analysis PRO, District Report PRO, Export Data PRO, API Docs. The panel on the guide says "We'll email you when one of these changes. **Manage your alerts**" and links here — and there is nothing about saved institutions or alerts on the page. Dead end.

**Professional (signed in).** `/guides/pro/building-a-peer-set` renders in full: three median stat cards (Overdraft $32.56, Monthly Maintenance $10.00, NSF $32.14), 25 paragraphs, "Watch out"/"Worth knowing" callouts, and "Open the peer tools" at the end. I did not find comparison *bars* — the DOM has no chart/SVG/progress elements; the numbers are cards and inline figures. `/fees/overdraft` shows the Bank vs Credit Union, Asset Tier, Federal Reserve District and State tables, no gate. `/pro` lands on `/pro/monitor` "Institutional Monitor", a different product skin (Hamilton nav: My Bank / Peer Compare / Scenarios / Reports & Briefs / Watchlist). The header says "Cascadia Community Bank – bank TIER_2" while the body says "No institutions tracked. Add one below." The copy ("Continuous surveillance … to preserve long-term recurring value", "Recurring Value preserves institutional permanence. HAMILTON STRATEGY PROTOCOL ✦") reads like placeholder strategy prose. On mobile the page overflows horizontally (566px wide on 375) and "STABLE" collides with the "0 new signals" counter.

## 4. Top five UX changes (by impact)
